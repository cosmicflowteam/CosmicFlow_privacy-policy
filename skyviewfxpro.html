This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application SkyviewFX Pro ("App"), developed and published by CosmicFlow Team.

SkyviewFX Pro provides weather forecasts, interactive weather maps, radar imagery, home screen widgets, optional live wallpaper features, and severe weather alerts. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be accessed or stored, how it is used, how we obtain your consent, and your choices.

Information We Collect

SkyviewFX Pro does not require account registration and does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, mailing address, or phone number.

Depending on how you use the App, the following information may be accessed or stored on your device:

  • Approximate or precise device location (only after you consent and grant Android permission)
  • Manually searched or saved city/location names and coordinates
  • Weather-related preferences (units, theme, widget settings, map layer choices)
  • Local app settings and disclosure/consent status stored on your device
  • Diagnostic and crash information that may be provided by Android or Google system services

CosmicFlow Team does not operate its own servers to store user accounts or personal profiles.

In-App Disclosure and Consent

Before you continue using location-based features, SkyviewFX Pro shows an in-app Location disclosure that explains:

  • What location data may be accessed (precise or approximate device location)
  • How location is used (local weather, radar/maps, and optional GPS widgets or live wallpaper)
  • That location is not sold or used for advertising
  • That you can skip GPS and pick a city manually

You must take an affirmative action (for example, tapping “I understand”) before proceeding. Separately, if you choose a GPS option such as Follow me or Use GPS once, the App shows another in-app disclosure immediately before the Android system location permission prompt. You may cancel and pick a city instead.

The App does not access device location until you grant the Android location permission (or until you provide a location by searching for a city). Navigating away from a disclosure screen without accepting is not treated as consent.

Location Information

Location is used only to provide local weather forecasts, radar, map layers, weather alerts, widgets, and live wallpaper information for your selected area.

You may choose how location is used within the App, including:

  • Following your device location (“Follow me”)
  • Capturing GPS once and saving a fixed location
  • Searching for and selecting a city manually (no GPS required)

Foreground location only. SkyviewFX Pro requests Android location permission for use while the App is in use (or when a feature you configured, such as a GPS widget refresh or live wallpaper update, needs a current position). The App does not request or use Android’s background location permission (ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION).

You may deny location access in Android settings and still use the App by selecting a location manually.

Sharing: Location coordinates may be included in requests to public weather and map providers solely to retrieve weather/map data for your chosen area. CosmicFlow Team does not sell location data and does not use location for advertising or ad tracking.

Weather Data Providers

SkyviewFX Pro obtains forecast and weather information from public third-party providers, including:

  • National Weather Service (NWS) — United States forecasts, observations, and alerts
  • MET Norway — international weather forecasts where NWS coverage is unavailable

Requests sent to these providers may include geographic coordinates necessary to retrieve weather information for your selected location. Those providers receive only the data needed to return weather results and are subject to their own privacy policies.

Radar, Maps, and Weather Imagery

The App displays weather maps and radar imagery using publicly available data services. Depending on your location and settings, these may include:

  • Iowa Environmental Mesonet (IEM) — U.S. NEXRAD radar mosaics
  • RainViewer — international or fallback radar composites
  • NOAA NDFD — forecast map layers such as temperature, precipitation chance, and cloud cover
  • NOAA Weather Prediction Center (WPC) — surface front analysis
  • National Hurricane Center (NHC) — tropical storm tracks, cones, and outlooks
  • CARTO / OpenStreetMap-style basemaps — basemap tiles used behind radar and map layers

Network access is required to download weather data, radar tiles, and map imagery. Map and radar requests may include coordinates for the area you are viewing.

Widgets and Live Wallpaper

SkyviewFX Pro may display forecast information, weather conditions, metrics, radar imagery, and selected locations through Android home screen widgets and optional live wallpaper features.

You can set each widget or the live wallpaper independently to:

  • GPS / Follow me — weather for your device location (requires location permission), or
  • A saved/fixed city — weather for a city you choose (no ongoing GPS needed)

App city search and wallpaper/widget GPS settings are separate. Searching a city in the main App does not change a GPS-configured widget or wallpaper to that city.

Widget and wallpaper preferences and cached forecast snapshots are stored locally on your device. These features do not publicly share your information.

Notifications

With your permission, the App may send local notifications for severe weather alerts from the National Weather Service when enabled in settings.

You can enable or disable notifications at any time through Android system settings or within the App.

Your Choices

  • Uninstall the App at any time.

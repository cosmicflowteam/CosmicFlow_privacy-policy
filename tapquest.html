Tap Quest

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Tap Quest ("the app") is a single-player arcade game. We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

Tap Quest stores game settings, best scores, game preferences, and purchase status locally on your device. When advertisements are shown, Google AdMob and Google Play services may collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location, and interaction data according to Google's privacy policies to deliver and measure advertisements.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Game settings, your best scores, your selected game preferences, and your purchase or entitlement status are stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. This information remains on your device unless included in your own device backup.

3. Advertising

The free version of Tap Quest displays advertisements through Google AdMob, including interstitial ads shown after some game sessions. Ads may use the Android Advertising ID. You can limit ad personalization through your Android device settings. Purchasing the optional Tap Quest Upgrade removes advertisements.

4. In-App Purchases

Tap Quest offers an optional one-time purchase called Tap Quest Upgrade using Google Play Billing. We do not receive, process, or store your payment information. All payment processing and purchase records are handled entirely by Google Play. For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Permissions

The app may use the following permissions:

  • Internet access (for advertisements)
  • Google Play Billing (for purchases)
  • Advertising ID (for personalized ads where permitted)

6. Children

Tap Quest does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13, except for information that may be processed by Google AdMob under Google's policies.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any material changes will be reflected in an updated version of the app and this page.

TeleZap Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What TeleZap does

TeleZap is a TV remote control app. It discovers TVs on your local network and sends remote-control commands (power, volume, channel, navigation) to them over your Wi-Fi network.

Data we collect

TeleZap does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All data stays on your device.

  • TV devices you add (name, IP address, MAC address) are stored locally in app storage.
  • Remote pairing tokens issued by your TV are stored locally so you don't have to re-approve the app.
  • App settings (theme, skin, preferences) are stored locally.

Network access

The app uses your local network to find TVs (SSDP/mDNS discovery and subnet scanning) and to send remote commands. It also uses Wake-on-LAN broadcast packets to power on supported TVs. No data leaves your local network except purchases handled by Google Play.

Permissions

  • Internet — required for TV communication.
  • Nearby Wi-Fi devices / Local network — required to discover TVs on your network.
  • Location (older Android versions) — required by the OS for local network discovery.

Purchases

Premium skins are unlocked via a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play. TeleZap never sees your payment information.

Third-party services

TeleZap does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The only third-party service involved is Google Play Billing for purchases.

Data retention & deletion

All data is stored on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all stored data. You can also clear saved devices from Settings → Devices.

Children

TeleZap does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the app's store listing.

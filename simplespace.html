Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

SimpleSpace ("the app") is a local storage cleaner and device optimizer. We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

SimpleSpace does not create accounts, collect email addresses, use advertising identifiers, include analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, or sell personal information.

To provide cleaning and optimization features, the app analyzes files, photos, videos, audio, installed apps, and app usage on your device. This analysis happens on your device. We do not collect or upload your personal files, photos, or app contents to any server we operate.

When you view details for an installed app, SimpleSpace may request publicly available listing information (such as description, developer name, genre, rating, and download count) from the Google Play Store web page for that app's package name. Google processes that request according to Google's privacy policies.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

App settings, language, theme, cleanup history, keep-list exclusions, recently deleted file records, hibernate preferences, storage scan caches, Play Store listing cache, and similar preferences are stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. This information remains on your device unless included in your own device backup.

3. Files, Photos, and App Data

SimpleSpace reads file and media metadata (such as name, size, date, and type) so you can review and free storage. Files you choose to delete or trash are handled on your device through Android system APIs. Recently deleted items may be kept in a local journal so you can restore them for a limited time.

The optional Clipboard screen reads the current clipboard only while that screen is open, so you can view or clear copied text. Clipboard contents are not uploaded.

4. Advertising

SimpleSpace does not display advertisements and does not use Google AdMob or the Android Advertising ID.

5. In-App Purchases

SimpleSpace does not offer in-app purchases and does not use Google Play Billing.

6. Accessibility Service

SimpleSpace may ask you to enable an optional Accessibility service so it can tap Force stop in Android Settings when you hibernate idle apps. This service is used only for that hibernation flow. It does not read passwords or other personal content, and it is not required to scan storage or delete files.

7. Permissions

The app may use the following permissions:

  • Storage and media access (to scan, preview, and clean files, photos, videos, and audio)
  • All-files access (on Android 11 and later, for a complete storage scan)
  • Usage access (to see which apps use storage and battery)
  • Query installed apps (to list apps you can review or uninstall)
  • Request uninstall (to start Android's uninstall flow for apps you select)
  • Internet and network state (to load public Play Store listing details)
  • Notifications (for optional alerts)
  • Accessibility (optional, to Force stop apps during hibernation)
  • Kill background processes (for Phone Boost)

8. Children

SimpleSpace is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any material changes will be reflected in an updated version of the app and this page.

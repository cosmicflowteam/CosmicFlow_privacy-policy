Privacy Policy

Last updated: Aug 2026

SlingQuest ("the app") is a single-player arcade climbing game. We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

SlingQuest stores game settings, best scores, climber choice, and purchase status locally on your device. When advertisements are shown, Google AdMob and Google Play services may collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location, and interaction data according to Google's privacy policies to deliver and measure advertisements.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Game settings, your best scores, your selected climber, and your purchase or entitlement status are stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. This information remains on your device unless included in your own device backup.

3. Advertising

The free version of SlingQuest displays advertisements through Google AdMob, including interstitial ads shown after some game sessions. Ads may use the Android Advertising ID. You can limit ad personalization through your Android device settings. Purchasing the optional SlingQuest Upgrade removes advertisements.

4. In-App Purchases

SlingQuest offers an optional one-time purchase called SlingQuest Upgrade using Google Play Billing. We do not receive, process, or store your payment information. All payment processing and purchase records are handled entirely by Google Play. For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Permissions

The app may use the following permissions:

  • Internet access (for advertisements)
  • Google Play Billing (for purchases)
  • Advertising ID (for personalized ads where permitted)

6. Children

SlingQuest does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13, except for information that may be processed by Google AdMob under Google's policies.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any material changes will be reflected in an updated version of the app and this page.

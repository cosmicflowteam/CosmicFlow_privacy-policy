LiveFX Wallpapers Pro
Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

LiveFX Wallpapers Pro ("the app") is a live wallpaper application that provides animated wallpapers and visual effects for Android devices. The app does not require an account to use its wallpaper features. We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

LiveFX Wallpapers Pro does not create accounts, collect email addresses, use advertising identifiers, include analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, or sell personal information.

The app stores wallpaper selections and customization preferences locally on your device. These settings are used only to provide and customize the wallpapers you select.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

LiveFX Wallpapers Pro may store information such as your selected wallpaper, wallpaper category, visual effects, animation settings, and other customization preferences locally on your device.

This information remains on your device unless it is included in your own device backup. The app does not require an account or cloud storage to use these features.

3. Live Wallpapers and Device Features

LiveFX Wallpapers Pro uses Android's live wallpaper functionality to display animated backgrounds and visual effects on your device.

Depending on the wallpaper selected, the app may use device capabilities such as animation rendering, motion sensors, or gyroscope data to create visual effects. Any such information is processed locally on the device and is not transmitted to us.

The app does not use device location to provide its wallpaper functionality.

4. Internet Access

Certain features or wallpaper content may require Internet access to download or retrieve content. When Internet access is used, the app does not intentionally transmit personal information such as your name, email address, contacts, or precise device location to us.

LiveFX Wallpapers Pro does not maintain user accounts or a personal cloud profile.

5. Advertising

LiveFX Wallpapers Pro does not display advertisements and does not use Google AdMob or the Android Advertising ID.

6. In-App Purchases

LiveFX Wallpapers Pro may offer premium wallpaper content or features through Google Play's billing system.

Purchases are processed by Google Play. The app does not receive or store your credit card number, bank account information, or other payment credentials. Google handles payment processing according to Google's own policies.

7. Permissions

Depending on the features and wallpapers installed, the app may use the following Android permissions or capabilities:

  • Internet access for downloading or retrieving wallpaper content
  • Live wallpaper service to display animated wallpapers
  • Motion or gyroscope sensors for supported interactive effects

Permissions and device capabilities are used only for the functionality described by the app.

8. Children

LiveFX Wallpapers Pro is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any material changes will be reflected in an updated version of the app and this page.
